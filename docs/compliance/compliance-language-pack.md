# Compliance Language Pack

This copy pack is intentionally conservative and aligned with the project plan.
Use it as the default source for onboarding, calculator, settings/legal, and store listing drafts.

## 1) Voice and framing rules

### Preferred language
Use words like:
- private tracker
- record-keeping
- logs
- reminders
- history
- user-entered information
- user-input calculations
- informational
- organization

### Avoid language like
- recommended dose
- ideal dose
- prescribed schedule
- protocol recommendation
- optimization
- treatment plan
- medical advice
- personalized guidance
- doctor replacement
- clinically proven results
- safest/best compound or dose

## 2) Onboarding copy

### Welcome headline options
- Private peptide and GLP-1 tracker
- Track routines privately
- Logs, reminders, and records in one place

### Welcome body
- Track routines, reminders, and history in one private app.
- No account is required for the MVP, and your records stay on this device.

### Disclaimer screen title
Medical and safety notice

### Disclaimer screen body
This app is for record-keeping, reminders, and informational calculations based on values you enter.
It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment guidance, or personalized dose recommendations.
Always use your own judgment and consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.

### Acceptance checkbox
- I understand this app is a tracking tool and not medical advice.

### Primary CTA

- I understand

### Optional secondary helper text
- You can review this notice again later in Settings.

## 7) Store listing copy

### Short description options
- Private peptide and GLP-1 tracker with logs, reminders, and history.
- Track routines privately with reminders, logs, and simple user-input math.

Keep your peptide and GLP-1 routines organized in one private tracker.

Use the app to:
- log routines and history
- manage reminders you create
- organize compounds and protocols
- use simple calculations based on values you enter
- keep records on-device without creating an account in the MVP

Important:
This app is for tracking, record-keeping, and informational calculations only.
It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment guidance, or personalized dose recommendations.

### Promotional bullets
- Private, local-first tracking
- Fast logging and simple reminders
- Organized routine history
- No account required in the MVP

### Store metadata guardrail
Before publishing, reject any store text that includes:
- recommend / recommended dose
- prescribe / prescription support
- treatment optimization
- personalized guidance
- medical outcomes claims
- efficacy or safety claims

## 8) Forbidden claims list
Never ship product copy that says or strongly implies:
- this app tells you the right dose
- this app suggests the best protocol
- this app improves treatment results
- this app replaces a doctor or clinic
- this app provides personalized medical guidance
- this app is clinically validated for dosing decisions

## 9) Reusable one-line disclaimer variants
- Tracking tool only. Not medical advice.
- Informational calculations only. Not a dose recommendation.
- For private records and reminders, not treatment guidance.
