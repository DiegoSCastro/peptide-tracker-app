## 1. Positioning north star

Primary positioning statement:
> A private tracker for peptide and GLP-1 routines with reminders, logs, and simple user-input math — built for record-keeping, not medical advice.

Store copy priorities:
1. Lead with privacy, tracking, reminders, and logs.
2. Frame calculator value as neutral math from user-entered values only.
3. Emphasize no-account / on-device convenience where accurate.
4. Avoid any language that implies treatment guidance, dose recommendations, optimization, or outcomes.

## 9. Compliance review checklist for final listing

Approve final listing only if all answers are yes:
- Does the title frame the app as a tracker, log, or reminder tool rather than an advisor?
- Does the short description avoid telling users what to take, how much to take, or when to take it?
- Does the full description clearly state that calculations are based on user-entered values?
- Does the listing avoid claims about treatment, efficacy, optimization, or better outcomes?
- Do the screenshots show app utility without clinical imagery or recommendation-like language?
- Are privacy claims limited to what is true in the release build?
- Is Pro value framed around convenience, organization, exports, and ad removal only?
